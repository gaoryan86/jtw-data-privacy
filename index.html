Privacy Policy — JTW Data

Last updated: October 18, 2026

JTW Data is a private, single-operator tool used only by the owner of the Lucky Plume Facebook Page and the @lucky.plume Instagram and Threads accounts. It is not offered to the public and no other people can sign in to it.

What the app accesses

Using the owner's own access tokens, the app publishes videos and comments to the owner's own Facebook Page, Instagram account and Threads account, and reads performance insights and comments on that content.

How data is used and stored

Insights and comment text are saved to the owner's private computer for content analysis only. The data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Comments made by other people on our posts may be stored as public text for reading and replying; no other personal information is collected.

Data deletion

To ask for any stored data that relates to you, including your comments, to be deleted, send a message to the Lucky Plume Facebook Page saying "JTW Data deletion". We delete it within 30 days and confirm in the same conversation.
